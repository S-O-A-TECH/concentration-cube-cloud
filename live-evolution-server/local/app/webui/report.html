How to use this screen — where a human decides

What the two tables mean

  • train (practice) — the score on data the AI saw and practiced on. Good scores here can be expected.
  • holdout (the real exam) — the score on data the AI has never seen. Base your adoption decision on this side. Good on train but bad on holdout means overfitting.
  • Each cell is before → after (current criteria → with the candidate applied). Higher is better.

Gate badge

  • Rule: all holdout states degrade within -2%p & the target state improves — the operations server decides.
  • The [Adopt] button turns on only when passed (the server also rejects a non-passed adoption with 409 — double defense).

When you press [Adopt]

  • All sessions are re-scored with the new criteria, and the interpretation criteria change immediately.
  • To undo, use [Rollback] in the generation history — two clicks reverts it.
  • A rejection (REJECTED) is not a failure — the reason is recorded and becomes input for the next generation.
