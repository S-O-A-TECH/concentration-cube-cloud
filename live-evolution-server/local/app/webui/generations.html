Generations

The canonical lineage is the operations server's param_sets — joined with this server's agent transcripts (agent_runs). Print it and it becomes a clinical document.

How to use this screen — the full record of what was tried and why it turned out that way

Lineage tree dot colors

  • ● adopted — a ring means the currently active criteria
  • ● candidate/passed — registered/passed but not yet adopted
  • ● rejected/failed — rejected/failed (also a valuable record)
  • ● rolled_back — reverted after adoption

What you can do

  • [View] — that generation's change diff, scores, and the full mission the agent actually received and its raw response (for reproducibility and audit).
  • [🖨 Print view] — output that becomes a clinical document/report as-is.
  • [Rollback] — only on the active (adopted) generation. After a 2-step confirm the previous generation becomes active again, and all sessions are re-scored with that criteria.
If a candidate shows "no local record", it was created outside this console (e.g. manual registration).
