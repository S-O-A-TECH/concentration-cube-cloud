Real-time validation session console

[Start] starts the device directly, and the button you press in sync with your instruction is the only ground truth. There is no post-hoc editing — if it fails, [Exclude from research] and redo the 5-minute session.

How to use this screen — ground truth (labels) is born only here
The subject and the administrator are in the same room, and the administrator presses the button at the same time as giving the verbal instruction. The button-click time becomes the ground truth (recorded by the device's session clock — not the PC clock).

What you need

  • The webcam proto must be running: .venv\Scripts\python run.py in the web_cam_version_prototype folder (the device badge is shown above)
  • 1 subject + a book. The session is 5 minutes (LAB-5) — short and frequent makes good data.

Running it (e.g. the default 5-min preset)

  • [Start] → calibration (subject gazes at the four corners of the book) → measurement begins
  • Say "focus and read from now!" and click [Focus] at the same time — then the board shows the next instruction and countdown in large text. There is no auto-marking — only the click at the moment you actually instructed is the truth.
  • [Pause/other] = restroom, cough, interruption — that bin is dropped from ground truth (excluded from evaluation).
  • If you misclick or the protocol breaks down → [Discard and restart]. It's only 5 minutes, so no pressure.

After finishing

  • Check the summary and [Save] — once per session, not editable. If in doubt, don't save — [Exclude from research] instead.
  • If there's a "sync warning" badge, check the timeline first.
  • Saved sessions appear at the top of the archive. Once 10 or more ground truths are gathered, Run evolution unlocks.
The device's real-time judgment is hidden by default (the subject may see the screen, and to keep the administrator's timing from being dragged by the machine) — turn on the debug display in Settings if needed.
